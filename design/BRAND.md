# No-halla brand

Write **No-halla**, pronounced “no-HAH-lah”. Keep the capital N and the hyphen. The tagline is **Valhalla can wait.** In Russian, **Вальхалла подождёт.**

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The gate | VPN tunnel |
| Trusted realm | Trusted IPv4, IPv6 or CIDR |
| Waiting at the gate | Selected apps are frozen |
| The gate holds | Protection is active on a trusted IP |
| The gate is open | Protection is paused or disabled |
| The watch | No-halla and its watchdog |

Use calm sentence case. Add lore in a supporting line, on About or in a notification title. Keep functional state labels and actions explicit. The notification body describes the event and available action. Avoid language about combat, death, sacrifice or blame.

**Selected icon.** The circular Shield & pause has a boss and two bars. At 16 and 32 px, the silhouette and bars carry recognition. At 128 px, the ridge and rim can be seen. At 512 and 1024 px, the 24 small ticks, boss and double rim carry texture. The alternative Shield & gate swaps the center bars for a doorway and loses clarity earlier at small sizes. Both are depicted in [brand-sheet.svg](exports/brand-sheet.svg).

Draw the menu bar glyph in one color at 18 pt with a 1.4 pt outline and no ornament. Render as a template so macOS supplies the foreground. The 1.5 pt onboarding lines describe the symmetric gate and horizontal fjord. Keep the geometric marks abstract. The native shell uses the deep fjord and raised group tones in dark mode. Reuse the 24 short marks in the 1 pt header divider and About. Bronze is decoration on the pill, selected tab and frozen row. The functional text, glyphs and primary actions keep their semantic colors. The onboarding and About artwork and compact empty-state vignette share the layered gate and fjord geometry.
